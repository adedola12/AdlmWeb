This guide is for everyone who uses ADLM Studio software, whether you have just heard of us or you are renewing for the third year. It walks you through creating your account, paying for a product, installing it, and looking after your licence, devices, invoices and support requests on adlmstudio.net. At the end there is a table of every ADLM product with a link to its own guide.

## Before you start

You need:

- An email address you can open now. We send a six-digit code to it before your account can be used.
- A WhatsApp number, with the country code (for example +2348012345678). It is required when you sign up.
- A web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Everything in this guide works in any modern browser.
- For the desktop products (QUIV, HERON, RateGen, Revit MEP, CIVIQ and Time Pro), a Windows 64-bit computer. These are installed with the [ADLM Installer Hub](/guides/installer-hub).

One ADLM account signs you in to every product, on every machine. The same email and password work on the website, in the Installer Hub and inside Revit, PlanSwift and the other host programs.

> **Tip:** Keep this guide open on your phone while you work on your computer. The steps are short so you can follow them side by side.

## Create your account

### Sign up with your email

1. Open adlmstudio.net and click **Sign up** in the top bar. On a phone, open the menu first.
2. Enter your **First name** and **Last name**.
3. Enter your **WhatsApp number (e.g. +2348012345678)**.
4. Enter your **Email**.
5. Enter a **Password**. It must be at least 8 characters and contain at least one letter and one number.
6. Click **Sign up**.

You are taken straight to the **Confirm your email** screen.

> **Note:** If the site says the user already exists, an account with that email (or the same name before the @) is already registered. Click **Login** instead and use **Forgot password?** if you need to.

### Confirm your email address

Your account cannot be used, and you cannot buy anything, until your email address is confirmed.

1. Open the email from ADLM Studio. It contains a six-digit code. It can take a minute to arrive, so check your spam or promotions folder too.
2. Type the six digits into the box on the **Confirm your email** screen.
3. Click **Confirm**.

The code works for 30 minutes. On the same screen you can:

- Click **Send a new code** if it has expired or never arrived.
- Click **Wrong address? Change it** if you mistyped your email, enter **The right email address**, then click **Send the code there**.
- Click **Sign out** to leave and come back later.

Until you confirm, a yellow bar across the top of every page reads "Confirm your email address to use your account." Click **Enter the code** in that bar to come back to this screen.

> **Important:** The desktop products also refuse to sign in until your email is confirmed. They tell you to sign in at adlmstudio.net, enter the code, and then try again.

### Sign up with Google, Microsoft or Autodesk

Under the sign-up form you may see buttons such as **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Microsoft** or **Continue with Autodesk**. Only the ones we have switched on are shown. Clicking one creates your ADLM account from that provider's account (or signs you in, if you already have one with the same email).

> **Important:** Google, Microsoft and Autodesk sign-in work on the website only. The Windows software always asks for an email and a password. After a social sign-in you are taken to your Profile and asked to **Set a password for the desktop software**. Do it straight away, otherwise you cannot open QUIV, HERON, RateGen, the Revit MEP plugin, Time Pro or CIVIQ. Your social sign-in keeps working on the website.

## Sign in

1. Click **Login** in the top bar.
2. Enter your **Email or username** and your **Password**. Click the eye icon to show or hide what you typed.
3. Click **Sign in**.

If you connected Google, Microsoft or Autodesk, you can click the matching **Continue with** button instead.

### Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your **Email or username** and click **Send code**.
3. Check your email for a six-digit code. It works for 10 minutes.
4. Type the code into **Enter 6-digit code**.
5. Type a **New password** (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number).
6. Click **Set new password**.
7. Sign in with the new password.

If the code does not arrive, click **Didn't get a code? Resend**.

### The codes we send you

ADLM Studio uses short six-digit codes in a few places. Each one is sent to you only, and each has a short life:

| Code | When you get it | Valid for |
|---|---|---|
| Email confirmation | After you sign up, or when you change the address | 30 minutes |
| Password reset | After you click **Forgot password?** | 10 minutes |
| Destructive-action code | Before deleting a project or locking a contract, if you turned this on (see below) | About 10 minutes |

> **Tip:** Never share a code with anyone. ADLM support does not need your codes or your password to help you.

### Extra protection for deleting projects

On your Profile, under **Security & Admin**, you can tick **Require an email code for destructive actions**. When it is on, we email you a six-digit code before you can delete a project or lock or unlock a contract. Untick it to turn it off.

## Your profile and WhatsApp number

There are two places to edit your details. Both save to the same account.

### On the Profile page

Click **Profile** in the top bar. Under **Account details** you can:

1. Click **Upload new photo** to set your picture.
2. Edit your **Username**, **First Name** and **Last Name**.
3. Enter your **Firm name** and **Location**.
4. Choose your **Pricing location (State)**. Your RateGen prices, and the prices in projects you price afterwards, use the zone your state belongs to. Click **View Prices** to see them.
5. Click **Save**.

> **Note:** If your name has been printed on a course certificate, it is locked. The page says so. Contact support if it needs correcting.

### In Account settings

Open your account area at `/manage` and click **Account settings** in the left-hand menu (see "The Manage area" below). The **Your profile** panel has **First name**, **Last name**, **Phone** and an optional **Display name**. Click **Save profile** when you are done.

- **Work email** cannot be changed here. The email is the account itself. To change it, contact support.
- **Phone** is your WhatsApp number. Enter it with the country code, for example +234 803 000 0000.
- **Rates and currency** lets you pick **Where you work** (your state). The **Geopolitical zone** fills itself in. **Currency** switches how prices are shown to you between NGN and USD. Click **Save location**.
- **The firm** holds your **Practice name** and **Address**. These are printed on invoices raised from now on. Click **Save firm details**.

### Verifying your WhatsApp number

If you see a **Verify on WhatsApp** button under **Phone** in Account settings:

1. Make sure the number in **Phone** is right, and click **Save profile** first. A changed number must be saved before it can be verified.
2. Click **Verify on WhatsApp**.
3. Open WhatsApp on your phone. A six-digit code arrives from ADLM Studio.
4. Type it into the **6-digit code** box and click **Confirm**.

When it works, the field reads **Verified on WhatsApp**. Click **Send again** if the code does not arrive. Changing your number later removes the verified mark until you verify the new one.

> **Note:** WhatsApp verification is being switched on gradually. If you do not see the button, there is nothing you need to do.

## Buying a product or subscription

You can buy from any product page or from the checkout page.

### From a product page

1. Click **Products** in the top bar and open a product.
2. Under **Choose your term**, pick how long you want. Longer terms show the percentage you save.
3. Click **Get started now**. You are taken to checkout with the product already chosen.

If a product shows **Notify me when available** instead, it is not on sale yet. Click it to be told when it is.

### At checkout

Click **Purchase** in the top bar to open the **Subscribe** page. You must be signed in, with your email confirmed.

1. At the top, choose your **Currency**: NGN or USD.
2. Choose **Purchase for**: **Personal** (one seat, one computer) or **Organization** (several seats for a firm).
3. In the **Products** list on the left, click a product.
4. Set the **Duration**. For monthly products, tap **1 mo**, **6 mo** or **12 mo**, or use the minus and plus buttons. Six and 12 months unlock better pricing.
5. If you chose **Organization**, set **Seats / users**. Some products have a minimum number of users for organisation licences; the page tells you.
6. If the product offers it, tick **First-time install** to add our one-time installation fee.
7. If you pay in NGN, you can add **Extra project storage** in blocks of 10 project slots.
8. Check the total at the bottom of the box and click **Add to order**.
9. Repeat for any other products. A green dot in the list marks products already in your order. To change one, click it, adjust it and click **Update order**. To take it out, click **Remove**.

Your order builds up in the **Summary** on the right.

### Buying for an organisation

When **Purchase for** is set to **Organization**, an **Organization details** box appears:

1. Enter the **Organization name**. It is required.
2. Optionally add an **Organization email** and **Phone**.
3. Prices are shown per seat. The seats you choose on each product are the number of users or computers your organisation needs.

In the same box you can tick **Need physical training for your office?**. Choose a **Training location**, and the location, duration and **Training cost** are shown. You can also tick **Also install BIM Softwares on office computers?**. The training date is agreed with you after your purchase is approved.

### Bundles and multi-month discounts

- Most products are cheaper per month when you buy 6 or 12 months at once. The saving is shown on the product page and is applied automatically at checkout.
- You can put several products in one order and pay for them together. Each appears as its own line in the **Summary**.

### Coupons

1. In the **Summary**, find **Discount coupon**.
2. Type your code in **Enter coupon code**.
3. Click **Apply**.

When the code is accepted, the summary shows **Applied:** with the code and the discount, and the **Discount** line is taken off your **Subtotal**. Any VAT is then added and the final **Total** shown. Add products to your order before applying a coupon.

> **Tip:** When a promotion is running, a coupon banner appears across the top of the public pages with the code.

### Auto-renewal

If you are paying in NGN, the **Summary** shows **Auto-renew my subscription**. Tick it before you pay. It only applies when you pay by card: we save the card and charge it at the price at that time shortly before your licence expires, so your access carries on without a new order. If a renewal is declined, you get an email and it is tried again before your access ends.

You can switch auto-renew on or off for each product, or remove the saved card, at any time (see "Saved card and auto-renewal").

### Paying

Click **Pay**. The **Complete your payment** box opens.

**Paying by card (NGN orders)**

1. Click **Pay with Card (Paystack)**.
2. Enter your card details in the Paystack window. Your bank may ask for an OTP.
3. When the payment succeeds you see **Payment successful! Your access has been activated.** and you are taken to your account area.

Your licence is active as soon as the card payment is confirmed. If you were debited but the page could not confirm it, wait a few minutes: the payment is confirmed automatically, and your dashboard updates.

> **Note:** Card payments are charged in Naira. Cards issued in Nigeria clear reliably. If you are paying from outside Nigeria, the site may suggest bank transfer instead. If you do hold a Nigerian card, click **I have a Nigerian card. Let me pay by card anyway**. On a USD order, click **Switch to NGN & pay by card** to pay by card.

**Paying by bank transfer**

1. In the **Complete your payment** box, note the **Account number**, **Account name** and **Bank**.
2. Make the transfer from your bank app.
3. Click **I have paid**.

A bank transfer is checked by our team before your licence is switched on. Your order shows **Awaiting admin approval** until then. If we sent you an order link by email, open it and click **Upload your receipt** to attach a photo or PDF of your transfer slip (up to 8MB). That speeds up approval.

### Quotations

If you need a figure for your firm's approval before you buy, build a quotation. You do not need to be signed in.

1. Open `/quote`, or on a phone choose **Get Quotation** from the menu.
2. Choose the **Currency** and **Billing** (**Yearly** or **Monthly**).
3. Under **Select Software Products**, click each product you need.
4. For each selected product, set **Number of PCs / Users**.
5. If you want training, tick **I need physical training at my office** and choose a location.
6. Read **Your Quotation** and the **Estimated Total**.
7. Click **Print Quote** to print or save it as a PDF, or click **Email Quote**, enter **Your name** and **Email address**, and click **Send**.
8. When you are ready, click **Proceed to Purchase**.

> **Note:** A quotation is an estimate. The final price is the one shown at checkout.

### Invoices and receipts

- **Orders.** Every purchase is listed on your Profile under **Orders, Invoices & Installations**, in the **Orders** tab, with its status: **Paid**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Awaiting admin approval**. Once an order is approved, click **Print / Download Receipt** to open the receipt, then print it or save it as a PDF.
- **Invoices.** Invoices raised for you by ADLM Studio are in the **Invoices** tab. Click **View Invoice** to open one, or **Download PDF** to save it. When an invoice is paid, **Download Receipt** appears beside it.
- **In the Manage area.** **Billing & invoices** lists the same invoices with **PDF** and, for paid ones, **Receipt** links.

> **Tip:** If ADLM sent an invoice to your email before you had an account, sign up with that same email. The invoice is linked to your new account automatically.

## Organisation seats and team members

### How seats work

- A **seat** is one licence for one computer. A seat activates against a machine, not a person.
- A **Personal** licence has one seat.
- An **Organization** licence has as many seats as you bought for each product.

To see your seats, open **Team** in the Manage area (the page is called **Team & seats**). It shows:

- **Seats per product**: how many seats each product has and how many are in use.
- **Machines**: each computer holding a seat, the products on it and when it was last used, with a **Free the seat** button.
- **Free activations**: **Seats owned**, **In use** and **Free right now**.

To add seats, click **Buy more seats** (or **Buy more**). You are taken to checkout. Installing on a new machine costs nothing extra while you have a free seat.

### Adding team members

Adding colleagues to one shared account is not available yet. Today, each person signs in with their own ADLM account, and a licence is bought against that account. If you need several people under one bill, click **Talk to us about a practice account** on the Team page and we set it up for you by hand.

> **Tip:** To work with a colleague on a single project, you do not need to share an account. Projects can be shared with a link; see the [ADLM Cloud guide](/guides/cloud).

## Devices and licence limits

Each seat can be active on one computer at a time. If you sign in to a product on a computer when all your seats are already in use, the product refuses and tells you which computer holds the seat. The message reads like this:

- "This QUIV licence is already in use on another computer..." (one seat), or
- "All 3 seats on this QUIV licence are in use..." (several seats)

followed by the name of the computer and when it was last used.

### Moving a licence to a new computer

You can do this yourself, without a ticket:

1. On any device, sign in at adlmstudio.net and open **Team** in the Manage area (or **Account settings**, under **Machines signed in**).
2. Find the old computer in the list.
3. Click **Free the seat** (on the Team page) or **Sign out** (in Account settings).
4. Confirm. The seat is freed at once. Nothing on the old computer is deleted; it simply stops being licensed at its next check-in.
5. Sign in to the product on the new computer.

> **Important:** Free the seat first, then install on the new machine. Reinstalling without freeing the seat uses up an activation that support then has to release for you.

### Things that count, and things that do not

- A rebuilt, reformatted or reimaged computer counts as a new device. Free the old entry first.
- Switching between Wi-Fi, ethernet or a VPN does **not** use up a seat. Activation is tied to the device, not the network.
- Changing your password signs your other machines out at their next check-in, so they will ask for the new password.

## Getting support

### Ask Ada first

The **Ask Ada** button sits in the corner of every page. Ada is ADLM's assistant: she answers questions about products, prices and training from ADLM's own information, and once you are signed in she can also answer about your own subscriptions and projects. She can hand you to a person on WhatsApp whenever you prefer.

### Open a support ticket

There are two ways to raise a ticket. Both need you to be signed in.

**From the Support page**

1. Go to `/support`, or click **Contact Support** under **Quick Links** on your dashboard.
2. Click **Raise a Technical Ticket**. The **Request technical help** page opens.
3. Enter a **Title**, for example "Can't open RateGen after update".
4. Describe the problem in **What's happening?**, including any error message.
5. Choose a **Category**: **Technical issue**, **Account / login**, **Billing / subscription**, **Feature request** or **General question**.
6. Choose **Which product / software?**.
7. Optionally click **Add images** to attach up to 5 screenshots, 2MB each.
8. If you would like us to fix it on your computer, install AnyDesk and paste your **AnyDesk address**.
9. Click **Submit request**.

Your tickets and their status are listed under **Your requests** on the same page. We reply by email.

**From the Manage area**

1. Open **Support** in the Manage area and click **Open a ticket**.
2. Choose **Which product** and, if it is about one computer, **Which machine**.
3. Enter a **Summary** and fill in **What happens, and what you expected**.
4. Optionally add your **AnyDesk address**.
5. Click **Send to support**.

A ticket opened here already carries your licences and machines, so we do not need to ask for them. Your tickets appear under **Your tickets**, with any reply from support.

> **Tip:** The two things that shorten a ticket most are the exact wording of any message you saw, and what you had just done when it appeared. A screenshot helps.

## Troubleshooting

### I never received my confirmation code

Wait a minute and check your spam or promotions folder. On the **Confirm your email** screen, click **Send a new code**. If the address is wrong, click **Wrong address? Change it**. Each code works for 30 minutes.

### The desktop software says my email is not confirmed

Sign in at adlmstudio.net, enter the six-digit code on the **Confirm your email** screen (click **Send a new code** if needed), then sign in to the product again.

### I signed up with Google and the software will not accept my sign-in

The Windows products need an email and a password. Open your Profile and use **Set a password for the desktop software**, then sign in to the product with your email and that password.

### My card was debited but my licence is not active

Card payments are usually confirmed within moments. If the page could not confirm it, the payment is confirmed automatically shortly afterwards. Refresh your dashboard after a few minutes. If it is still not active, open a ticket with the **Billing / subscription** category or message us on WhatsApp.

### My card was declined

Cards issued outside Nigeria are often declined because the charge is in Naira. Pay by bank transfer instead, then click **I have paid** and upload your receipt.

### My order says Awaiting admin approval

Bank transfers are checked by our team before the licence is switched on. Make sure you clicked **I have paid**, and upload your transfer receipt on the order page if you have the link. Contact support on WhatsApp if it has been more than a working day.

### My coupon was not accepted

Add at least one product to your order first, then apply the code. Check the spelling. Coupons can expire or apply only to certain products; the message under the box says why.

### A product says the licence is in use on another computer

All your seats are taken. Free the old computer's seat from **Team** or **Account settings** in the Manage area, then sign in again. If you need more computers at once, buy more seats.

### I replaced my computer and cannot activate

A new or rebuilt computer counts as a new device. Free the old one under **Team** in the Manage area, then sign in on the new one.

### The ADLM panel is missing in Revit or PlanSwift after installing

Revit or PlanSwift was open during the install. Close it and start it again.

### The website says "Failed to fetch"

Run the network check at `/network-check` and send us the results with the reference code. It is usually something on your office network blocking part of the website.

### I cannot change my email address

The email is your account. Contact support to change it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a separate account for each product?

No. One ADLM account signs in to every product, on every machine.

### Can I use one licence on two computers?

Not at the same time. A personal licence has one seat, which is one computer. To use two computers at once, buy an organisation licence with two seats. To move between computers, free the seat on the old one first.

### Can I pay in US dollars?

You can view prices and place an order in USD. Card payments are charged in Naira, so to pay by card switch the order to NGN; otherwise pay by bank transfer.

### What happens when my subscription ends?

The licence stops working at the end of the period you paid for. Your rate library, projects and takeoffs stay in your account, and are there again when you renew. Click **Manage** on your subscription to renew.

### How do I renew?

Turn on auto-renew and pay by card, and it renews itself. Otherwise, click **Manage** on the subscription on your dashboard, or buy the product again at checkout.

### Where are my receipts?

On your Profile under **Orders, Invoices & Installations**, and under **Billing & invoices** in the Manage area.

### Can I add my colleagues to my account?

Not yet. Each person uses their own ADLM account. For several people under one bill, ask us about a practice account from the **Team** page.

### Will unsubscribing stop my renewal notices?

No. Receipts, licence activations, renewal notices and support replies always arrive.
