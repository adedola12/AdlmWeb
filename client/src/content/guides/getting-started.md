This guide is for everyone who uses ADLM Studio software, whether you have just heard of us or you are renewing for the third year. It walks you through creating your account, paying for a product, installing it, and looking after your licence, devices, invoices and support requests on adlmstudio.net. It describes the website as it is on 5 October 2026, after the new design went live on 1 October. At the end there is a table of every ADLM product with a link to its own guide.

## What's new in October 2026

- **A new website design.** The public pages, sign-in and sign-up have a new look. Once you sign in you land on your **Overview** (`/manage`), with one menu down the left side of every signed-in page. The old `/dashboard` address opens the Overview too.
- **Every menu item opens its own page.** Since 4 October, **Products & seats**, **Team**, **Billing & invoices**, **Downloads**, **Support**, **Guides & docs** and **Account settings** each open their own page under `/manage`. Before that, some of them opened older pages such as your Profile.
- **Sign in and sign up in one place.** **Sign in** and **Sign up** sit at the top right of every public page, with **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Microsoft** or **Continue with Autodesk** under each form.
- **Software comes from the Installer Hub only.** The only file you download from the website is the ADLM Installer Hub. The Hub installs every product your licence covers.
- **WhatsApp verification.** Confirm your WhatsApp number with a six-digit code sent on WhatsApp, from **Account settings**.
- **Sign-up checks your email can receive our code.** An address that can never receive mail is refused at sign-up, so you are not left waiting for a code that cannot arrive.
- **Ada, on every page.** **Ask Ada** is at the top of the signed-in menu as well as the round button in the corner. Signed in, she can answer about your own licences and projects.
- **Choose how the site looks.** **Appearance** in **Account settings** offers **Light**, **Dark blue**, **Black** or **Match my device**.

## Before you start

You need:

- An email address you can open now. We send a six-digit code to it before your account can be used.
- A WhatsApp number, with the country code (for example +2348012345678). It is required when you sign up.
- A web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Everything in this guide works in any modern browser.
- For the desktop products (QUIV, HERON, Rate Gen, Revit MEP, CIVIQ and Time Pro), a Windows 64-bit computer. These are installed with the [ADLM Installer Hub](/guides/installer-hub).

One ADLM account signs you in to every product, on every machine. The same email and password work on the website, in the Installer Hub and inside Revit, PlanSwift and the other host programs.

## Create your account

### Sign up with your email

1. Open adlmstudio.net and click **Sign up** at the top right. On a phone, open the menu first.
2. On **Create your ADLM account**, enter your **First name** and **Last name**.
3. Enter your **Email**.
4. Enter your **WhatsApp number**, with the country code.
5. Enter a **Password**. It must be at least 8 characters and contain at least one letter and one number.
6. Click **Create account**. It reads **Creating your account…** while it works.

You are taken straight to the **Confirm your email** screen. By creating an account you agree to the **Terms** and **Privacy Policy** linked under the button.

> **Note:** If the site says the user already exists, an account with that email is already registered. Click **Sign in** instead and use **Forgot your password?** if you need to. If it says your email cannot receive our code, check the spelling or use another address.

### Confirm your email address

Your account cannot be used, and you cannot buy anything, until your email address is confirmed.

1. Open the email from ADLM Studio. It contains a six-digit code. It can take a minute to arrive, so check your spam or promotions folder too.
2. Type the six digits into the box on the **Confirm your email** screen.
3. Click **Confirm**.

The code works for 30 minutes. On the same screen you can:

- Click **Send a new code** if it has expired or never arrived.
- Click **Wrong address? Change it** if you mistyped your email, enter **The right email address**, then click **Send the code there**.
- Click **Sign out** to leave and come back later.

Once confirmed, you arrive on your **Overview**. Until you confirm, a bar across the top of every page reads "Confirm your email address to use your account." Click **Enter the code** in that bar to come back to this screen.

> **Important:** The desktop products also refuse to sign in until your email is confirmed. They tell you to sign in at adlmstudio.net, enter the code, and then try again.

### Sign up with Google, Microsoft or Autodesk

Under the sign-up form you may see **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Microsoft** or **Continue with Autodesk**. Only the ones we have switched on are shown. Clicking one creates your ADLM account from that provider's account, already confirmed, or signs you in if you already have one with the same email.

> **Important:** Google, Microsoft and Autodesk sign-in work on the website only. The Windows software always asks for an email and a password. After a social sign-in, open your **Profile** and use **Set a password for the desktop software**. Do it straight away, otherwise you cannot open QUIV, HERON, Rate Gen, the Revit MEP plugin, Time Pro or CIVIQ.

## Sign in

![The sign-in page, with email and password or Google and Microsoft sign-in.](shot:web-sign-in.png)

1. Click **Sign in** at the top right of any public page.
2. Enter your **Email or username** and your **Password**.
3. Leave **Keep me signed in** ticked on your own computer. Untick it on a shared one.
4. Click **Sign in**.

If you connected Google, Microsoft or Autodesk, you can click the matching **Continue with** button instead. New to ADLM? Click **Create an ADLM account** on the same page.

Some accounts are asked for a second step. If you see **Enter your code**, type the **6-digit code** we emailed you and click **Sign in**.

### Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot your password?**. The **Reset your password** form opens.
2. Enter your **Email** and click **Send the code**.
3. Check your email for a six-digit code. It works for 10 minutes.
4. Type the code into **6-digit code**.
5. Type a **New password** (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number).
6. Click **Set the password**. The page says "Password updated. You can now sign in."
7. Sign in with the new password.

Click **Back to sign in** to leave the form. If you are still stuck, click **Contact support** under **Trouble signing in?**.

### The codes we send you

| Code | When you get it | Valid for |
|---|---|---|
| Email confirmation | After you sign up, or when you change the address | 30 minutes |
| Password reset | After you click **Forgot your password?** | 10 minutes |
| WhatsApp verification | When you click **Verify on WhatsApp** | A few minutes |
| Destructive-action code | Before deleting a project or locking a contract, if you turned this on | About 10 minutes |

> **Tip:** Never share a code with anyone. ADLM support does not need your codes or your password to help you.

### Extra protection for deleting projects

On your **Profile**, under **Security & Admin**, tick **Require an email code for destructive actions**. When it is on, we email you a six-digit code before you can delete a project or lock or unlock a contract. Untick it to turn it off.

## Buying a product or subscription

### See the prices

**Pricing** at the top of the public pages (`/pricing`) shows every product's price. Prices are per PC: one ADLM account can hold several seats, and you assign them to machines yourself.

![The Pricing page, with See the prices and Build a quotation.](shot:web-pricing.jpg)

- Click **See the prices** to jump to the price list.
- Click **Explore QUIV** (or the matching button for another product) to open that product's page.
- Click **Build a quotation** for a printable quotation (see [Quotations](#quotations)).

### From a product page

1. Click **Products** at the top of the public pages and open a product.
2. Under **Choose your term**, pick how long you want. Longer terms show the percentage you save.
3. Click **Get started now**. You are taken to checkout with the product already chosen.

If a product shows **Notify me when available** instead, it is not on sale yet. Click it to be told when it is.

### At checkout

The checkout page is called **Subscribe** (`/purchase`). You must be signed in, with your email confirmed.

1. At the top, choose your **Currency**: **NGN (₦)** or **USD ($)**.
2. Choose **Purchase for**: **Personal** (one seat, one computer) or **Organization** (several seats for a firm).
3. In the products list, click a product.
4. Set the **Duration**. "Tip: 6 and 12 months unlock better pricing."
5. If you chose **Organization**, set **Seats / users**. Some products have a minimum number of users for organisation licences; the page tells you.
6. If the product offers it, tick **First-time install** to add our one-time installation fee.
7. If you pay in NGN, you can add extra project storage in blocks of 10 project slots.
8. Check the total and click **Add to order**.
9. Repeat for any other products. To change one, click it, adjust it and click **Update order**.

Your order builds up in the **Summary**.

### Buying for an organisation

When **Purchase for** is set to **Organization**, an **Organization details** box appears:

1. Enter the **Organization name**. It is required.
2. Optionally add an organisation email and phone.
3. Prices are shown per seat. The seats you choose on each product are the number of users or computers your organisation needs.

In the same box you can ask for physical training at your office: choose a training location, and the **Location:**, duration and **Training cost:** are shown. You can also add **BIM Software Installation** on your **Office computers setup**. The training date is agreed with you after your purchase is approved.

### Coupons

1. In the **Summary**, find **Discount coupon**.
2. Type your code in **Enter coupon code**.
3. Click **Apply**.

When the code is accepted, the **Discount** line is taken off your **Subtotal**, any VAT is added and the final **Total** shown. Add products to your order before applying a coupon.

### Auto-renewal

If you are paying in NGN, the **Summary** shows **Auto-renew my subscription**. Tick it before you pay. It only applies when you pay by card: we save the card and charge it at the price at that time shortly before your licence expires. If a renewal is declined, you get an email and it is tried again before your access ends.

### Paying

Click **Pay**. The **Complete your payment** box opens.

**Paying by card (NGN orders)**

1. Click **Pay with Card (Paystack)**.
2. Enter your card details in the Paystack window. Your bank may ask for an OTP.
3. When the payment succeeds your access is activated and you are taken to your account area.

Your licence is active as soon as the card payment is confirmed. If you were debited but the page could not confirm it, wait a few minutes: the payment is confirmed automatically.

> **Note:** Card payments are charged in Naira, and **Nigerian-issued cards** clear reliably. On a USD order, click **Switch to NGN & pay by card** to pay by card. If you hold a Nigerian card and the page suggests a transfer, click **I have a Nigerian card. Switch to NGN & pay by card**.

**Paying by bank transfer**

1. In the **Complete your payment** box, note the **Account number**, **Account name** and **Bank**.
2. Make the transfer from your bank app.
3. Click **I have paid**.

A bank transfer is checked by our team before your licence is switched on. Your order shows **Awaiting admin approval** until then. If we sent you an order link by email, open it and click **Upload your receipt** to attach a photo or PDF of your transfer slip. That speeds up approval.

### Quotations

If you need a figure for your firm's approval before you buy, build a quotation at `/quote`. You do not need to be signed in.

1. Open `/quote`, or click **Build a quotation** on the Pricing page.
2. Choose the currency and billing (yearly or monthly).
3. Choose each product you need and the number of PCs or users.
4. Add training at your office if you want it.
5. Print or save the quotation, or have it emailed to you.

> **Note:** A quotation is an estimate. The final price is the one shown at checkout.

### Invoices and receipts

- **Orders.** Every purchase is on your **Profile** under **Orders, Invoices & Installations**, in the **Orders** tab, with its status: **Paid**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Awaiting admin approval**. Once an order is approved, click **Print / Download Receipt**.
- **Invoices.** Invoices raised for you by ADLM Studio are in the **Invoices** tab. Click **View Invoice** to open one, or **Download PDF** to save it. When an invoice is paid, **Download Receipt** appears beside it.
- **On the Billing & invoices page** (`/manage/billing`), **Invoices** lists the same invoices with **PDF** and **Receipt** links.

> **Tip:** If ADLM sent an invoice to your email before you had an account, sign up with that same email. The invoice is linked to your new account automatically.

## Saved card and auto-renewal

When you pay by card with auto-renew ticked, the card is saved for renewals. Manage it on your **Profile** under **Billing & auto-renewal**.

- The card shows as its type and last four digits, with the expiry date.
- Each subscription has its own **Auto-renew** tick box. Untick it to stop that product renewing.
- If a renewal failed, the line says so with the reason.
- To stop all renewals, click **Remove card**, then **Yes, remove**. The page says "Saved card removed. Auto-renew is off for all products."

If you have no saved card, pay any order by card and it is saved for renewals.

## Installing your software

Once you have an active licence, install everything with the ADLM Installer Hub.

1. On your **Overview**, click **Download for Windows** under **ADLM Installer Hub**. (Or open **Downloads** and click **Download the Installer Hub**.)
2. Run the downloaded file on your Windows computer.
3. Sign in to the Hub with your ADLM email and password.
4. Install the products your licence covers.

The full walkthrough is in the [ADLM Installer Hub guide](/guides/installer-hub), and **Watch the walkthrough** on the Overview plays the video.

> **Important:** Close Revit and PlanSwift before installing a plugin. If the host program is open during the install, the ADLM panel does not appear until you close it and start it again.

> **Note:** The website only offers the Installer Hub. Individual product packages are installed by the Hub, which keeps your licence, version and updates in step. An account with no active licence sees no Hub download; buy a product first.

If you bought a **First-time install**, the **Installations** tab on your Profile shows its status and next steps. You can install it yourself with the Hub, or send your AnyDesk address to support so we can install it for you remotely.

## Licences, seats and devices

### How seats work

- A **seat** is one licence for one computer. A seat activates against a machine, not a person.
- A **Personal** licence has one seat.
- An **Organization** licence has as many seats as you bought for each product.

To see your seats, open **Team** (`/manage/team`, titled **Team & seats**), or click **Seats** on the Products page. It shows:

- **Seats per product**: how many seats each product has and how many are in use.
- **Machines**: each computer holding a seat, the products on it and when it was last used, with a **Free the seat** button.
- **Free activations**: seats owned, in use and free right now.

To add seats, click **Buy more seats**. Installing on a new machine costs nothing extra while you have a free seat.

### When all your seats are in use

Each seat can be active on one computer at a time. If you sign in to a product on a computer when all your seats are in use, the product refuses and tells you which computer holds the seat, for example "This QUIV licence is already in use on another computer..." or "All 3 seats on this QUIV licence are in use...", with the computer's name and when it was last used.

### Moving a licence to a new computer

You can do this yourself, without a ticket:

1. Sign in at adlmstudio.net and open **Team** (`/manage/team`), or **Machines signed in** in **Account settings**.
2. Find the old computer in the list.
3. Click **Free the seat** (on the Team page) or **Sign out** (in Account settings).
4. Confirm. The seat is freed at once. Nothing on the old computer is deleted; it simply stops being licensed at its next check-in.
5. Sign in to the product on the new computer.

You can also release a seat from the Installer Hub on the computer itself. See the [Installer Hub guide](/guides/installer-hub).

> **Important:** Free the seat first, then install on the new machine. Reinstalling without freeing the seat uses up an activation that support then has to release for you.

### Things that count, and things that do not

- A rebuilt, reformatted or reimaged computer counts as a new device. Free the old entry first.
- Switching between Wi-Fi, ethernet or a VPN does **not** use up a seat.
- Changing your password signs your other machines out at their next check-in, so they will ask for the new password.

Each person signs in with their own ADLM account; to work on one project together, share it (see [Sharing, team and Ada](/guides/cloud-sharing)).

## Getting support

### Ask Ada first

Click **Ask Ada** at the top of the menu or the round button in the corner of any page. Ada answers questions about products, prices and training from ADLM's own information, and once you are signed in she can also answer about your own licences and projects. She can hand you to a person on WhatsApp whenever you prefer.

### Open a support ticket

Click **Support** in the menu to open the **Support** page (`/manage/support`).

![The Support page: open a ticket, your tickets, answers and how to reach a person.](shot:dash-support.png)

1. Under **Open a ticket**, choose **Which product** (or **Account, seats or billing**) and, if it is about one computer, **Which machine**.
2. Enter a **Summary**, for example "The Revit panel does not appear after installing".
3. Under **What happens, and what you expected**, give the steps, the exact message if there is one, and when it started.
4. Optionally add your AnyDesk address, only if you want somebody to take a look on your machine.
5. Click **Send to support**. The page says "Sent. Support has your licences, versions and machines with it."

Your tickets appear under **Your tickets**, with their status (**Open**, **In progress**, **Scheduled**, **Resolved** or **Closed**) and any reply **From support:**. **Answers first** has short answers to common questions, such as moving a licence to a new laptop or a missing panel after installing. **Reach a human** has WhatsApp, email and our hours. We reply by email.

> **Note:** The older **Request technical help** form at `/support/request` still works, and your tickets from it are kept.

## Troubleshooting

### I never received my confirmation code

Wait a minute and check your spam or promotions folder. On the **Confirm your email** screen, click **Send a new code**. If the address is wrong, click **Wrong address? Change it**. Each code works for 30 minutes.

### Sign-up says my email cannot receive the code

The address has a typing mistake, or its domain cannot receive email. Check the spelling, or sign up with another address.

### The desktop software says my email is not confirmed

Sign in at adlmstudio.net, enter the six-digit code on the **Confirm your email** screen (click **Send a new code** if needed), then sign in to the product again.

### I signed up with Google and the software will not accept my sign-in

The Windows products need an email and a password. Open your **Profile** and use **Set a password for the desktop software**, then sign in to the product with your email and that password.

### I cannot find my orders or auto-renewal settings

Invoices and the next charge are on **Billing & invoices** in the menu. Your full order history and the **Auto-renew** tick boxes for each product are on your **Profile** (`/profile`), under **Orders, Invoices & Installations** and **Billing & auto-renewal**.

### My card was debited but my licence is not active

Card payments are usually confirmed within moments. If the page could not confirm it, the payment is confirmed automatically shortly afterwards. Refresh your Overview after a few minutes. If it is still not active, open a ticket with the **Billing / subscription** category or message us on WhatsApp.

### My card was declined

Cards issued outside Nigeria are often declined because the charge is in Naira. Pay by bank transfer instead, then click **I have paid** and upload your receipt.

### My order says Awaiting admin approval

Bank transfers are checked by our team before the licence is switched on. Make sure you clicked **I have paid**, and upload your transfer receipt on the order page if you have the link. Contact support on WhatsApp if it has been more than a working day.

### A product says the licence is in use on another computer

All your seats are taken. Free the old computer's seat from **Team** or **Machines signed in**, then sign in again. If you need more computers at once, buy more seats.

### The ADLM panel is missing in Revit or PlanSwift after installing

Revit or PlanSwift was open during the install. Close it and start it again.

### The website says "Failed to fetch"

Run the network check at `/network-check` and send us the results with the reference code. It is usually something on your office network blocking part of the website.

### I cannot change my email address

The email is your account. Contact support to change it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a separate account for each product?

No. One ADLM account signs in to every product, on every machine.

### Can I use one licence on two computers?

Not at the same time. A personal licence has one seat, which is one computer. To use two computers at once, buy an organisation licence with two seats. To move between computers, free the seat on the old one first.

### What happens when my subscription ends?

The licence stops working at the end of the period you paid for. Your rate library, projects and takeoffs stay in your account, and are there again when you renew.

### How do I renew?

Turn on auto-renew and pay by card, and it renews itself. Otherwise, buy the product again at checkout, or follow the link in **Needs your attention** on your Overview.

### Where are my receipts?

On your **Profile** under **Orders, Invoices & Installations**, and on the **Billing & invoices** page at `/manage/billing`.

### Where did the old Dashboard go?

It has been replaced by your **Overview** at `/manage`. The `/dashboard` address still works and opens the Overview.

### Will unsubscribing stop my renewal notices?

No. Receipts, licence activations, renewal notices and support replies always arrive.

### Who do I contact if I am stuck?

Ask Ada, open a ticket from **Support** in the menu, or message us on WhatsApp on +234 810 650 3524, Monday to Friday, 9 to 6 WAT.
