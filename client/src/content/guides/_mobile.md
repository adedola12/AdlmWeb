ADLM Cloud is the mobile companion to your ADLM account. It does not measure drawings or price bills: that work stays in QUIV, HERON, Rate Gen and the other desktop products. What the app does is answer the questions you have when you are away from your desk: "Is my licence still active?", "When does it renew?", "Has my renewal gone through?", "What did the team change in the last update?" and "Where is my support ticket?".

There is no separate mobile licence and no second account. You sign in with the same ADLM account you use on the website and in the Installer Hub, and the app shows what that account owns.

## Before you start

### What you need

- An ADLM account. If you can sign in at [adlmstudio.net](/login), you can sign in to the app with the same details.
- An Android phone with an internet connection. Wi-Fi or mobile data both work.
- A few megabytes of free space for the app.

> **Note:** The app is free. It is not a product you buy, and it does not appear in the product catalogue.

### Does it use one of my seats?

No. Your desktop products are activated per computer, and each activation uses a seat. Signing in to the phone app does not activate anything, so it does not use a seat and does not change the device count you see on your licences.

### Where to get it

The app is not yet listed on Google Play or the App Store. For now, Android users download it directly from the ADLM website. An iPhone version is planned but is not available yet.

## Install the app on Android

Because the app comes from the ADLM website rather than Google Play, Android will ask you to confirm that you trust the download. This is normal for any app installed outside the Play Store.

1. On your phone, open [adlmstudio.net](/) in Chrome or your usual browser.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page and tap **Download Mobile App now**.
3. Wait for the file to finish downloading. It is an Android app file (ending in `.apk`).
4. Tap the downloaded file, from the notification or from your **Downloads** folder.
5. If Android says your browser is not allowed to install unknown apps, tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
6. Tap **Install**.
7. When it finishes, tap **Open**, or find **ADLM Cloud** in your app drawer.

> **Important:** Only install the app from the official ADLM website. Do not install copies sent to you on WhatsApp or by email, because ADLM cannot vouch for them.

> **Tip:** The exact wording of the Android security prompts depends on your phone's make and Android version. If you cannot find the setting, search your phone's Settings for "install unknown apps".

### Updating the app

Apps installed outside Google Play do not update themselves. When ADLM releases a new version, download it again from the website and install it over the top. You do not need to uninstall first, and you stay signed in.

## Sign in

When you open the app for the first time you see the **Welcome back** screen.

1. Type your email address or your username in **Email or username**.
2. Type your password in **Password**.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

The **Sign in** button stays greyed out until both boxes are filled in.

### If your account uses a sign-in code

Some accounts have two-step sign-in turned on. If yours does, after you tap **Sign in** the screen changes to **Enter your code** and tells you where the code was sent.

1. Open the email from ADLM and find the 6-digit code.
2. Type it into the **6-digit code** box.
3. Tap **Verify code**.

If you typed the wrong password or want to start again, tap **Back to sign in**.

### Forgotten password

The app has no password reset of its own. Reset it on the website:

1. Go to [adlmstudio.net/login](/login).
2. Tap **Forgot password?** and follow the email.
3. Come back to the app and sign in with the new password.

### Staying signed in

After a successful sign-in, the app remembers you. When you open it again it signs you back in automatically, as long as you have an internet connection at that moment. You normally only see the sign-in screen again if you sign out, if you have not used the app for about a month, or if you open it with no connection (see [Offline behaviour](#offline-behaviour)).

## Finding your way around

Along the bottom of the screen are five tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your licences, how long each has left, and anything that needs your attention |
| **Projects** | A read-only list of your takeoff projects and PM Tracker projects |
| **Learning** | Your training enrolments and session dates |
| **Billing** | Your saved card and the renewal settings for each subscription |
| **Profile** | Your account details, Support, What's New and **Sign out** |

On every screen, a small signal-bars indicator sits at the top right. It shows how good your connection to ADLM is. See [The network indicator](#the-network-indicator).

To refresh any list, pull the screen down and let go.

The app follows your phone's light or dark setting automatically.

## Home

Home is the screen to check first. It answers one question: am I covered, and for how long?

### The banner and quick figures

At the top you see **Hi** and your name, and how long you have been a member. Below it are two figures:

- **Active plans**: how many of your licences are currently active.
- **Orders**: how many orders you have placed with ADLM.

The small exit icon at the top right of the banner signs you out.

### Attention cards

If something needs action, a card appears just under the figures. Tap the card to go straight to that product's renewal screen. There are two kinds:

- **Expiring soon with auto-renew off.** A product with 14 days or fewer left and auto-renew turned off shows the number of days left and the message **Auto-renew is off. Turn it on so it doesn't lapse.**
- **Renewal failed.** If ADLM tried to renew a subscription and the charge did not go through, the card reads "*product* renewal failed" in red, with the reason (for example, the card was declined).

> **Important:** A failed renewal does not fix itself. If you see a red renewal card, open it and sort out the card on file before your access ends.

### My subscriptions

Under **My subscriptions** there is one card for each licence on your account. Each card shows:

- The product name (for example QUIV, HERON, MEP, Civil 3D or RateGen).
- A status label: **Active**, **Active · 23d left** (the number of days left), or **Expired**. The label is green when you have plenty of time, amber at 14 days or fewer, and red when expired.
- **Renews / expires** and the date, or **Expired** and the date it ended.
- Whether it is a **Personal license** or an organisation licence (the organisation's name is shown).
- How many devices are using it out of how many seats, for example **1/3 devices**.
- How it is billed, where that applies.

If you have no licences yet, you see **No subscriptions yet**.

The **What's New** link beside the **My subscriptions** heading opens the latest product updates.

## Billing

The **Billing** tab shows **Your card, subscriptions and renewals**.

### Payment method

This card shows the bank card ADLM has on file for renewals: the card type, the last four digits, the expiry date, the bank and the date it was added.

You cannot type a new card into the app. A card is saved automatically the first time you pay for something by card on the ADLM website. If no card is saved, you see **No card saved** and a **Manage on the web** button, which opens your dashboard on the ADLM website in your phone's browser.

#### Removing your saved card

1. Tap **Remove card**.
2. Read the message: removing the card switches auto-renew off for every product, because renewals cannot be charged without a card.
3. Tap **Remove** to confirm, or **Keep card** to cancel.

### Subscriptions

Under **Subscriptions** there is one card per product you own. Each card shows:

- A label with the days left: **N days left**, **1 day left**, **Expires today**, **Expired** or **Inactive**. The colour is green with more than 30 days, amber between 8 and 30 days, and red at 7 days or fewer.
- Either "Auto-renews" with the date and how often (for example every 12 months), or "Expires" with the date and "auto-renew off".
- How many seats the subscription has.
- **Last renewal failed** and the reason, if the most recent renewal did not go through.

Tap a card, or its **Manage renewal** link, to open the renewal screen for that product.

If you own nothing that can be renewed, you see **Nothing to renew yet**.

## Turn on auto-renew

The renewal screen is where you keep a licence from lapsing. Reach it from an attention card on **Home**, or from **Manage renewal** on the **Billing** tab.

At the top you see the product, its days-left label, and either **Next charge** (if auto-renew is on) or **Access ends** (if it is off) with the date. If the last renewal failed, the reason is shown here too.

### Switch auto-renew on or off

1. Under **Auto-renew**, find **Renew automatically**.
2. Tap the switch to turn it on. It turns orange.
3. Wait for the message **Auto-renew is on.**

With auto-renew on, ADLM charges your saved card before your access ends, so your licence does not run out in the middle of a job. Nothing is charged in the app itself, and nothing is charged at the moment you flip the switch: the charge happens on the renewal date.

To stop renewing, tap the switch again. You see **Auto-renew is off.** and your access continues until the **Access ends** date.

### Choose how long each renewal lasts

Once auto-renew is on, **RENEWAL PERIOD** appears with four choices: **1m**, **3m**, **6m** and **12m** (months). Tap the one you want. The selected period is outlined in orange.

The line under the choices shows which card will be charged.

### If you have no card saved

If you try to turn on auto-renew without a saved card, the app shows **Add a card to turn on auto-renew**. Tap **Open on the web**, complete a card payment on the ADLM website, then come back to the app and turn auto-renew on.

> **Note:** Buying a new product, or adding a seat, is done on the ADLM website, not in the app. See [Products](/products).

## Projects

The **Projects** tab is a **Read-only view of your takeoff projects**. You cannot open, edit or delete a project here; do that in the desktop product that created it.

Projects are grouped under the product they came from (QUIV, HERON, MEP or Civil 3D). Each card shows:

- The project name.
- How many items it contains.
- The date it was last updated.
- A link icon, if you have turned on public sharing for that project.

If you have PM Tracker projects, they appear under **PM Tracker** with three counters: tasks, risks and issues. Risks turn amber and issues turn red when there are any.

If you have not saved any projects to ADLM Cloud, you see **No projects yet**.

## Learning

The **Learning** tab (headed **Trainings** on the screen) lists **Your course enrollments and upcoming sessions**. Each card shows:

- The training title.
- **Paid** in green once your payment is confirmed, otherwise your enrolment status.
- The date, or date range, of the training.
- The venue, for physical trainings.
- When you submitted your enrolment form, if you have.

If you have not enrolled in anything, you see **No enrollments yet**. To enrol in a training, use the [Trainings](/trainings) page on the website.

## Profile

The **Profile** tab shows your name, email address and your picture or initials. Under **Details** you see your **Username**, **Firm**, **WhatsApp** number, **State**, **Zone** and **Location**.

### Edit your profile

1. Tap **Edit profile**.
2. Change any of **First name**, **Last name**, **Username**, **Firm**, **WhatsApp** or **Location**.
3. Tap **Save changes**.

If the username you chose is already taken, the app tells you and keeps your changes on screen so you can pick another.

> **Note:** Your state and zone set the prices Rate Gen uses, so they cannot be changed in the app. Change them on the website.

### More

Under **More** you find **Support** and **What's New**. Both are described below.

### Sign out

Tap **Sign out** at the bottom of the Profile tab. You can also use the exit icon at the top of **Home**.

## Support

Open **Profile** and tap **Support** to see your tickets and raise a new one.

### Raise a ticket

1. Tap **New support ticket**.
2. Type a short title, for example "Can't open my HERON project".
3. Describe the problem in the large box: what you were doing, what you expected and what happened.
4. If you are happy for ADLM to connect to your computer to help, type your AnyDesk address in **AnyDesk address (optional)**.
5. Tap **Submit ticket**.

**Submit ticket** stays greyed out until you have filled in both the title and the description. Tap **Cancel** to close the form without sending.

> **Tip:** Your AnyDesk address is the number shown in the AnyDesk app on your computer, not your phone. Open AnyDesk on the computer that has the problem and copy the number from there.

### Follow your tickets

Under **My tickets**, each ticket shows its title, the date you opened it, and its status: open, in progress (amber) or resolved or closed (green). Pull down to refresh the list.

## What's New

**What's New** shows the latest release of each ADLM product: its version number, the release date, a one-line highlight and the changes grouped as **New**, **Improved** and **Fixed**. Open it from the **What's New** link on **Home** or from **Profile**.

For the full release history, see [What's New](/whats-new) on the website.

## The network indicator

The small pill at the top right of every screen shows the quality of your connection to ADLM. It works the same way as the indicator in the website and the desktop products, so four bars mean the same thing everywhere.

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| 4 green bars, **Excellent** | ADLM answers quickly. Everything loads straight away. |
| 3 green bars, **Good** | A normal connection. |
| 2 amber bars, **Fair** | Usable, but larger lists take a moment. |
| 1 red bar, **Poor** | Very slow. Loading and saving will be slow. |
| 1 amber bar, **No server** | Your phone has internet, but the ADLM server is not answering. |
| Bars with a red line through, **Offline** | Your phone has no internet connection. |
| **Checking** | The app is testing the connection. |

The bars measure how long the ADLM server takes to answer, not just your Wi-Fi or mobile signal. A full Wi-Fi signal to a router with no working internet will still show **Offline** or **No server**.

- **Tap** the pill to check again straight away.
- **Press and hold** the pill to see the details: the connection quality and response time, whether you are on Wi-Fi or mobile data, and when it was last checked.

The app checks about every 20 seconds while connected, and more often while offline so it notices quickly when you are back. It stops checking while the app is in the background, so it does not use your data or battery when you are not looking at it.

## Offline behaviour

The app needs an internet connection. It does not keep a copy of your information on the phone, so every screen loads fresh from ADLM each time you open it.

What happens when you have no connection:

- **Opening the app with no connection** takes you to the sign-in screen, because the app cannot confirm your sign-in. Your account is fine. Reconnect, close the app and open it again, and it signs you back in.
- **Signing in with no connection** fails with an error message.
- **Moving between tabs with no connection** shows an error message in place of the list. Reconnect, then pull the screen down to refresh.
- **Changing a setting with no connection** (auto-renew, renewal period, profile, a new ticket) does not save. The app shows an error. Nothing is queued to send later, so try again once you are back online.

> **Tip:** Check the network indicator first. If it shows **Offline** or **No server**, the problem is the connection, not your account.

## Notifications

The app does not send notifications to your phone. It does not alert you when a licence is about to expire or when a renewal fails.

To stay on top of renewals:

- Open the app now and then and look at **Home**. Anything expiring within 14 days without auto-renew, and any failed renewal, appears at the top.
- Turn on auto-renew for the products you rely on every day.

## Troubleshooting

### "The app keeps showing the sign-in screen when I open it"

The app restores your sign-in when it opens, and that needs internet. If you opened it with no connection, it shows the sign-in screen. Reconnect, close the app fully and open it again. If you are still asked to sign in, sign in again; this also happens after about a month without using the app.

### "It says my details are wrong but they work on the website"

- Check you are typing the same email or username you use on the website, with no space at the end.
- Passwords are case-sensitive. Check Caps Lock on your keyboard.
- If you have forgotten your password, reset it at [adlmstudio.net/login](/login) using **Forgot password?**.

### "I never received my sign-in code"

Check your spam or junk folder, and check the address hinted on the **Enter your code** screen is one you can reach. If you still have nothing after a few minutes, tap **Back to sign in** and sign in again to get a new code.

### "Every screen shows an error instead of my information"

Look at the network indicator.

- **Offline**: turn on Wi-Fi or mobile data, then pull down to refresh.
- **No server**: your internet works but ADLM is not answering. Wait a few minutes and try again. If it continues, contact support from the website.
- **Poor**: the connection is very slow. Move to a better signal or Wi-Fi, then pull down to refresh.

### "My licence shows as Expired but I have paid"

Pull down on **Home** to refresh. If it still shows **Expired**, payment may not have been matched to your account yet. Check your dashboard on the website and raise a support ticket with your payment reference.

### "I can't turn on auto-renew"

If you see **Add a card to turn on auto-renew**, there is no card on file. Tap **Open on the web** and make a card payment on the website; the card is saved as part of that payment. Then come back and turn auto-renew on.

### "My renewal failed"

The reason is shown on the red card on **Home** and on the product's renewal screen. The most common cause is a declined or expired card. Make a new card payment on the website so a working card is saved, then check that auto-renew is still on for the product.

### "Android won't let me install the app"

Android blocks apps from outside Google Play until you allow them. When prompted, open **Settings** and allow your browser (or file manager) to install unknown apps, then tap the file again. If you see "App not installed", make sure you have enough free storage and that the download finished completely, then download it again.

### "My project isn't in the Projects tab"

The tab only lists projects that have been saved to ADLM Cloud from a desktop product. A project kept only on your computer does not appear. Pull down to refresh after saving.

### "I don't see the app on the App Store"

The iPhone version is not available yet, and the Android app is not on Google Play yet. Android users can download it from the ADLM website.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to pay for the app?

No. The app is free and uses the same account as your desktop products.

### Can I buy a product or add a seat in the app?

No. Buying, adding seats and paying invoices are done on the ADLM website. The app opens the website in your browser where needed.

### Can I take off quantities or edit a bill on my phone?

No. Measuring and pricing stay in the desktop products. The app shows your projects read-only.

### Is my card number stored in the app?

No. The app only shows the card type, last four digits and expiry date of the card ADLM has on file. You cannot enter a card in the app.

### Does turning on auto-renew charge me straight away?

No. It tells ADLM to renew the subscription on its renewal date, using your saved card. You can turn it off at any time before then.

### Can more than one person in my firm use the app?

Yes. Each person signs in with their own ADLM account and sees the licences assigned to that account, including any organisation licence they belong to.

### Will the app work on my iPhone?

Not yet. An iPhone version is planned.

### Where do I get help if the app isn't working?

Raise a ticket from **Profile** then **Support**. If you cannot sign in at all, use the [Support](/support) page on the website.
