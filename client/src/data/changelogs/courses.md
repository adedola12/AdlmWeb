## 2.0 — September 2026 — Your learning, rebuilt

My learning, the course player, assignments and certificates now live in the new ADLM Studio design, with quizzes inside each lecture and a free library drawn from the whole ADLM YouTube channel.

### ✨ New

- **A new home for your learning.** My learning gathers your courses, firm videos and user guides. The course player keeps the lecture, recap, assignment and quiz together.
- **Quizzes at the point they are about.** Each lecture's questions now pause the video at the moment the point is made, with the answers shuffled.
- **Assignments with due dates and marks.** See what is to do, due soon, overdue, submitted and marked. Submit PDF, Word, Excel or image files, and read your tutor's feedback and mark in the same place.
- **Certificates you can prove.** Claim your certificate in three steps, choose a light or dark finish, and anyone who scans its QR code can confirm it is genuine at adlmstudio.net/certificate.
- **The whole ADLM YouTube channel, organised.** Free lessons include every long-form ADLM video, shelved by software, and product pages show recommended videos for that product.
- **Your firm's own video library.** Recordings made for your organisation appear in My learning.

### 🔧 Improved

- **Steadier video.** The player recovers from a dropped connection, and high-resolution recordings are no longer shown blurry.
- **Certificates print your name in a proper script font.**
