## 2026.10 — October 2026 — Take every product, pay less

Subscribe to every ADLM desktop product together and each subscription costs 5% less on monthly and 6-month plans, and 10% less on yearly plans, at checkout and at every renewal while you hold all of them.

### ✨ New

- **The all-products bundle.** Put QUIV, SERVIQ, HERON, Rate Gen and Time Pro in one order and each subscription is discounted: 5% under a year, 10% for a year or more. If you already hold all five, the same saving applies when they renew. Install fees are charged in full.

## 2026.09 — September 2026 — The new ADLM Cloud workspace

Your signed-in screens now sit in the new ADLM Studio design: one side rail that takes you between your projects, the programme, the rate library, your learning and your account. Alongside it come a project workspace whose totals add up line for line, read-only sample projects to learn from, and Ada in her new look.

### ✨ New

- **One home for your account.** Manage brings Overview, Products, Team & seats, Billing, Downloads, Guides, Settings and Support together. The overview shows which products are active, how many seats are in use, your next charge worked out from your real renewal dates, and anything that needs your attention.
- **Free a seat yourself.** Team & seats and Settings list every computer your licences are activated on. Sign one out to free its seat for a new laptop, with no support ticket and no waiting.
- **Billing you can switch.** Billing prices each licence monthly and yearly from the live price list and shows what you save on yearly. **Switch to yearly** sets the renewal cycle across all your active licences.
- **Settings that save.** Set your profile photo, your pricing state for rates and currency, and which emails you receive. Under Appearance, choose Light, Dark blue, Black or Match my device.
- **Support that already knows your setup.** A ticket raised from Manage → Support carries your licences, software versions and computers, so nobody has to ask you for them first.
- **Work overview.** One dashboard across your QUIV, HERON, Revit MEP, CIVIQ and ArchiCAD projects. **Pick up where you left off** reopens the exact project, tab and bill line you were last on.
- **Projects by tool.** Each tool has its own page of projects showing each job's stage, from measured to priced, contracted, certified and final account.
- **A programme built from your bill.** Work → Programme sequences the trades from the quantities your plugins already measured, so no durations are typed. Add or remove a gang and the whole chain moves. AI estimates each trade's gang output from your bill's own wording; every estimate shows its basis and stays editable.
- **The build-up behind every rate.** Work → Rate library opens any rate into its materials, labour, plant, overhead and profit. Edit a rate into your own copy or build a custom rate from scratch.
- **Learning samples in every product.** Every subscriber can open fully worked, read-only sample projects in QUIV, HERON, Revit MEP, CIVIQ and QUIV for ArchiCAD, each with a bill, a budget, a locked contract, certificates, variations and a programme.
- **Your firm's own videos.** Recordings made for your organisation now have their own library in My learning and on your overview.
- **The ADLM Software Complete Guide.** The full illustrated guide to the whole suite can be downloaded from What's New.
- **Network signal indicator.** The app bar shows the strength of your connection, so you can tell a slow network from a slow page.

### 🔧 Improved

- **The Bill's Summary adds up.** PC sums and provisional sums are now their own named groups, and the totals row adds the Bill's own columns. The estimated total now agrees with the contract sum frozen at lock, so on some projects it reads lower than before: the old figure was counting sums and preliminaries twice.
- **Valuations in one place.** The Valuation tab now holds certificates, variations and the final account. Variations carry an approval status, and only approved ones reach the contract figures.
- **A buy schedule you can tick off.** Materials can be marked as bought, and the lead time you set is saved with the project.
- **Picking a Rate Gen rate prices the whole line.** Choosing a rate on a bill line fills that line's material, labour and plant in the Budget, and reproduces the rate exact to the kobo.
- **Plant is plant.** Plant lines in rates and budgets are kept as plant everywhere, no longer folded into labour or counted as profit.
- **Material quantities match real practice.** Cement, sand and granite per m³, blinding, mortar, formwork nails, fill, DPM, mesh, tiles and rebar waste were recalibrated against about 580 real Nigerian QS bills.
- **Ada's new look.** Ada matches the new design, formats her answers cleanly, offers suggested questions, and can tell you which of your projects have a 3D model.
- **One computer, one seat.** The Installer Hub and QUIV now count your PC as the same machine, so updating one no longer locks the other out.
- **Confirm your email.** New accounts confirm their address with a six-digit code, so invoices and receipts reach a real inbox.

### 🐛 Fixed

- **Re-saving from a plugin wiped your Budget work.** Re-syncing a project from QUIV or HERON cleared materials marked as bought, buy-schedule dates, suppliers and rates typed on the web. All of it now survives a re-save.
- **Large firms could not sign in.** Accounts with many licensed seats got "Failed to fetch" on every signed-in page and in the Installer Hub. They now sign in normally.
- **Shared projects showed money they should hide.** A collaborator without a Rate Gen subscription could see a job's totals and download priced exports. That now follows the same rule as the project page, and a view-only collaborator can no longer overwrite rates.
- **Wrong standard descriptions in BoQ exports,** such as slab edge formwork billed as soffit formwork and ground beams counted twice, now land on the right lines.
- **Long CIVIQ roads opened as a thin grey line** in the 3D viewer. They now open on the first stretch, looking along the road.
- **Certificates on merged contracts** count towards Certified to date again, and a locked merged project can be saved.
- **The Privacy, Terms and Licensing pages** showed "Page not found". They are now published.

## 2026.08 — August 2026 — Sign in with Google, pricing by state, Excel bill & budget, and Ada checks your rates

Sign in with the account you already use, price every rate for your own state, take your bill and budget out to Excel the way you issue them, and let Ada check a bill before it goes out.

### ✨ New

- **Sign in with Google or Microsoft.** Use your work Google or Microsoft account on the sign-in and sign-up pages, and connect or disconnect them from your profile.
- **Prices for your state.** Rates are now priced for each of the 36 states and the FCT. Pick your pricing state in your profile and Rate Gen follows it.
- **Your own prices, per location.** In the web rate library you can set your own price for an item in each location you work in, and record your own specification against a rate.
- **Excel Bill & Budget export.** Export a project as it actually stands: the Bill of Quantities with its own sections, preliminaries, provisional sums and variations, a Bill Summary, and separate Material and Labour Schedules.
- **Bills in the format QSs issue.** Several buildings in one job, a sheet per structure rolling into one General Summary, preliminaries you can price, and milestone bills you can price, value and pay against.
- **Material & labour schedules from an imported bill.** An Excel bill with no schedule of its own now gets one, without moving your bill total.
- **Merged projects.** Measure the architectural and structural models separately, then combine them on the web into one project with one bill, one budget and one valuation.
- **Ada checks your bill.** Signed in, Ada can check your rates against the market line by line, find duplicates, wrong units, implausible quantities and rate outliers, and suggest a build-up for a rate.
- **Rate Gen for a single user.** Organisations can now buy Rate Gen for one seat.
- **User guides in your Learning area.** Your dashboard shows the guides for the products you own.

### 🔧 Improved

- **Product pages rebuilt.** Each product page shows what the product does, what it costs and what works with it. The products list can be searched, sorted and filtered.
- **Dollar prices follow the exchange rate.** Every USD price is the naira price at the day's exchange rate.
- **Ada is more reliable.** She now runs on ADLM's own AWS setup.
- **Better link previews** when you share an ADLM page on WhatsApp or social media.

### 🐛 Fixed

- **Excel export from the BoQ tab returned a server error.** It works again.
- **Invoice emails** listed products by internal code at ₦0. They now show the right products and amounts.
- **ArchiCAD projects** now appear on your dashboard and open correctly.

## 2026.07 — July 2026 — Card payments, auto-renewal, reports & Ada

A big month on the web: pay by card and let your subscription renew itself, download receipts and PDF reports, follow every change on a project, and ask Ada anything.

### ✨ New

- **Pay by card.** Card checkout is live alongside bank transfer, so a subscription can be activated in minutes instead of waiting on a transfer to be confirmed.
- **Subscriptions that renew themselves.** Tick auto-renew at checkout, or turn it on later in Profile → Billing. Your card is charged automatically before your licence expires and the entitlement is extended without a support ticket. You can see the saved card, switch auto-renew off, or remove the card at any time — and if a renewal is declined you get an email and it retries before your access lapses.
- **Downloadable receipts.** Every paid invoice can now be downloaded as a receipt PDF from your invoices page or your account activity feed.
- **Project, PM & Management PDF reports.** Generate a report from any open project — the project report, the PM report, or a management report across your whole portfolio — preview it in the app, then download the PDF.
- **Project activity log.** Profile → Project Activity shows a full trail of everything that has happened on your projects: creations, contract locks, variations, rate and budget edits, certificates, final accounts, model uploads, collaborator changes and PM schedule updates. It exports as a branded, printable PDF.
- **Ada, your AI assistant.** Ada replaces the old help bot: she answers questions about the products and pricing grounded in the real catalogue, and — once you are signed in — about your own projects and subscriptions. She can hand you over to a human on WhatsApp whenever you would rather talk to us.
- **Extra project storage slots.** Buy additional project slots in blocks of ten for any product straight from the purchase page, with the price shown live before you pay.
- **Excel BoQ import for QUIV.** Turn an existing Excel Bill of Quantities into a full QUIV project — it lands on your projects page with the whole budget, valuation, variation, PC-sum and dashboard pipeline behind it. The importer reads real-world QS workbooks (multiple bill sheets, section headings, preambles, lump-sum preliminaries) as well as the downloadable ADLM template, and re-importing a newer copy of the same workbook updates the project in place without losing your procurement marks or completion history. Available on request for accounts with a live QUIV subscription.
- **QUIV for ArchiCAD workspace.** A new web workspace for ArchiCAD takeoffs — Bill of Quantities, element detail, budget dashboard, unit switching and versioned exports.
- **User guides on your dashboard.** Four illustrated PDF guides — the Installer Hub, QUIV for Revit, ADLM Rate Gen, and the combined Installer Hub & ADLM Heron book — are now published on your dashboard and linked from your welcome email.

### 🔧 Improved

- **The portfolio dashboard now shows all of your work.** It was only reading four project types, so HERON, Civil and every materials project were invisible in the rollup. All of them are now included, with the same cost and valuation totals.
- **Buying from outside Nigeria.** Card charges are processed in Naira, which most foreign cards decline — so buyers outside Nigeria are now detected automatically, shown USD pricing, and led with bank transfer instead of hitting a card error. The card route is still there behind an explicit opt-in for anyone holding a Nigerian card.
- **A tidier projects list.** Schedule-only PM tracker projects no longer clutter the takeoffs list, and the sidebar has been reordered around how the products are actually used.
- **Faster help with sign-in problems.** When a plugin sign-in is rejected for a device mismatch, the server now records exactly what happened, so support can resolve it without asking you for screenshots.

### 🐛 Fixed

- **Purchases now grant exactly the duration you paid for**, and a yearly-billed course is capped at exactly one year.
- **Dashboard project counts** were wrong for some products, and a storage bar was showing on products that hold no projects.
- **Your cart survives sign-in.** A restored cart or a `?product=` link now reopens the configurator with your selections intact instead of being wiped, and edits made in the configurator apply to items already in your order straight away.
- **Budgets reflect completed BoQ items**, and the daily valuation log now rebuilds measured items from the project's own state, so valuations reconcile.
- **Nobody is locked out by the old device fingerprint.** The migration that moves a licence from the old device identity to the new one no longer expires on a fixed date — it stays open until each licence has moved across.
